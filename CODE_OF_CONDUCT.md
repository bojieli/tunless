# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, technically honest, and generous with context. Debate designs
and evidence without attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in
project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to keep the project safe and productive. Enforcement decisions consider
severity, pattern, impact, and the safety of affected people. Do not use public
issues to disclose private personal information. Use GitHub's private contact
and reporting mechanisms when confidentiality is necessary.

This code applies to repositories, reviews, discussions, project events, and
public representation of the project. Good-faith technical disagreement and
responsible security research are welcome.
